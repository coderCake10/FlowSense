The attached list identified the ESP-MESH paper as **Alshammari, Almutairi, and Alghamdi (2022)**. The matrix uses the publisher’s author list and should be cited as **Khan et al. (2022)**. The Holtgrave et al. (2025) entry was duplicated and is listed once.

## Synthesis for FlowSense

The sources collectively support six design foundations for FlowSense. First, indoor navigation should be represented as a structured spatial and graph problem rather than as an unstructured 3D image alone [1] [3] [17] [18]. Second, PWA caching and user-experience evaluation are relevant to the mobile handoff and should be assessed through both performance checks and the approved user survey [2] [15] [16]. Third, BLE can support indoor positioning, passive presence sensing, and occupancy estimation, but accuracy and privacy must be validated in the actual AUF environment [7] [9] [10] [12]. Fourth, ESP32 and MQTT are technically relevant for distributed hardware telemetry, although FlowSense requires its own power, range, packet reliability, and anomaly thresholds to be tested [4] [5] [8] [11] [13]. Fifth, passwordless authentication should be designed around adoption, recovery, usability, and administrative policy rather than authentication technology alone [6] [14] [19]. Sixth, the major research gap is the absence of an integrated, AUF-specific evaluation that combines 3D indoor wayfinding, map annotation, BLE-triggered mobile handoff, MQTT/ESP32 monitoring, PWA usability, and administrative decision support in one system.
