## Synthesis

Across the 63 sources, the literature supports FlowSense as an integrated indoor wayfinding system combining spatial data, graph-based routing, 3D visualization, kiosk and PWA interaction, BLE or WiFi sensing, ESP32 and MQTT telemetry, privacy-aware analytics, and passwordless administration. The central research gap is the limited availability of an AUF-specific evaluation that combines these layers in one operational campus system.
